- Are authored controls backend-neutral?

- Are edges/impulses retained and consumed correctly?
- Are pause, step, completion, and reset unambiguous?
- Are pointer spaces and outside-viewport behavior explicit?
- Are dead zones and combination deterministic?
- Can providers/mappings mutate runtime?
- Do behaviors emit normal intents?
- Are semantic frames replay authority?
- Are compatibility/equivalence checks strict?
- Does evidence avoid implying physical mouse/controller support?
- Can later adapters, remapping, multiplayer, rollback, and save/load extend the design?
